04 / Research

Chemical questions. Computational approaches.

Research foundations in computational chemistry and chemical informatics.

Clemson University · 2017–2023

Protein allostery & dynamics

Molecular dynamics and trajectory analysis using NAMD, CHARMM, and Bio3D; mutual-information classification of mutational effects; Keras/TensorFlow neural networks with regularization; stochastic, Gaussian mixture, and Cox proportional hazards models.

FDA · DARS · ORISE Fellowship · 2016–2017

Pharmacophore & toxicophore screening

Recommender systems and singular value decomposition for screening; NLP and semantic analysis of clinical text for toxicological endpoints; evaluation of (Q)SAR methods and study data tabulation models under departmental SOP and GCP standards.
