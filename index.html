Research Technology + Scientific Informatics

SourceAware

Integrated Exposure Analytics Platform

An environmental and exposure intelligence platform that brings monitoring, meteorological, facility, geographic, and community-context data into a traceable analytical environment.

Documented contributions: architecture and development, multi-source data integration, analytical pipelines, and visualization.

Scope and delivery status

The CV documents platform design and implemented data pipelines and dashboards, not production deployment. Automated ingestion and QA/QC, geospatial integration, coverage and monitoring-context assessment, and reproducible reporting describe the intended scope; their individual delivery status is not verified here. Experimental, planned, and proposed extensions are not presented as built capabilities.

Scientific Computing

Chemical Fingerprint and Exposure Analysis

Computational analysis of multivariate chemical profiles associated with secondhand vaping, combining chemical characterization, cheminformatics, defensible machine-learning validation, and modeled exposure assessment.

Documented contributions: reproducible VOC mixture-profiling pipelines evaluating feature stability and model performance.

Methods and interpretation

Volatile organic compound (VOC) profiles are chemical evidence; modeled exposure estimates are not direct measurements. Nested validation and permutation testing are evaluation methods for this area; their completed use is not verified here. This work concerns chemistry and exposure science, not clinical prediction or diagnosis.

Scientific Informatics + Scientific Computing

Chemical Hazard and Evidence Intelligence

Reproducible workflows that connect chemical identity, structure, physicochemical properties, hazard classifications, toxicological evidence, and transparent prioritization.

Documented contributions: chemical identifier reconciliation, descriptor calculation, integrated hazard characterization, and automated analytical reporting. Toxicophore screening also draws on the FDA fellowship.

Evidence boundaries

Evidence provenance, uncertainty, and missing-data visibility are central to interpreting these workflows. A calculated descriptor or toxicophore match is not itself a measured toxicological outcome. No compound lists, rankings, or internal reports are published here.

Research Technology + Scientific Informatics

Longitudinal Research Infrastructure

Research infrastructure designed to support repeated participant data collection, longitudinal study operations, secure data integration, metadata governance, provenance, and reproducible analysis.

Design scope: participant-facing systems, repeated data collection, access control, and integration of environmental, behavioral, biological, and clinical data.

Design, not a deployment claim

The supplied graph identifies a longitudinal research effort and governance needs. It does not establish implementation or deployment status. The broader integration scope is a design direction, not a claim that these data streams are collected or connected in production. No participant information or internal protocols are included.

Scientific Informatics

Scientific Knowledge Engineering

Methods for systematically discovering, extracting, organizing, connecting, and reviewing scientific information while preserving links between claims, source material, and expert interpretation.

Method scope: API-based acquisition, text and data mining, metadata normalization, semantic retrieval, knowledge graphs, and evidence synthesis.

Human review and method maturity

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), research recommendation and tracking, and AI-assisted extraction are methodological directions, not verified deployed products. Retrieved sources require expert review; generated text and inferred relationships are not independent scientific evidence.

05 / About

A scientific foundation.
A systems perspective.

Army chemical operations grounded my approach in preparedness and structured decisions. Chemistry and mathematics at Rutgers gave that approach a quantitative foundation; an FDA ORISE fellowship connected it to regulatory science, chemical informatics, and careful assessment of scientific data.

Doctoral research in computational biophysical chemistry at Clemson brought molecular modeling, protein dynamics, and statistical methods into that practice.
