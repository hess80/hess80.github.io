Research Technology + Scientific Informatics

SourceAware

Integrated Exposure Analytics Platform

An environmental and exposure intelligence platform that brings monitoring, meteorological, facility, geographic, and community context data into a traceable analytical environment.

Documented contributions: platform architecture and development; automated multi-source data ingestion and QA/QC; geospatial integration and visualization; monitoring coverage and context assessment; reusable analytical and reporting workflows.

Built capabilities and extensions

Built: multi-source exposure pipelines, data quality checks, spatial views, monitoring and community context, analytical outputs, and dashboards. Experimental/evaluated: AI assisted retrieval and reporting methods; generated narratives are intended to summarize validated platform outputs, not provide independent evidence or conclusions. Planned: active event and sensor network inputs and additional exposure modules are future capacity, not completed features. No confidential deployment details or unpublished results are included.

Computational Science

Chemical Fingerprint and Exposure Analysis

Computational analysis of multivariate chemical profiles associated with secondhand exposures, combining chemical characterization, cheminformatics, defensible machine learning validation, and modeled exposure assessment.

Documented contributions: reproducible pipelines for VOC mixture profiling, feature stability, model evaluation, and transparent analytical reporting.

Methods and interpretation

The biosketch documents nested cross-validation, permutation testing, and comparisons with null and feature benchmarks within exposure science modeling workflows. VOC profiles are chemical evidence; modeled exposure estimates are not direct measurements. This work concerns chemical characterization and exposure science, not clinical prediction or diagnosis. No manuscript in progress or unpublished finding is presented as a research output.

Scientific Informatics + Computational Science

Chemical Hazard and Evidence Intelligence

Reproducible workflows that connect chemical identity, structure, physicochemical properties, hazard classifications, toxicological evidence, and transparent prioritization.

Documented contributions: chemical identity reconciliation, descriptor calculation, integrated hazard characterization, and traceable comparison and reporting workflows. Toxicophore screening also draws on the FDA fellowship.

Evidence boundaries

Documented workflow outputs include comparative analyses, tables, logs, visualizations, and reports that retain source detail. Evidence provenance, uncertainty, and missing data visibility matter: a calculated descriptor, predicted property, or toxicophore match is not a measured toxicological outcome. No compound lists, unpublished rankings, or internal reports are published here.

Research Technology + Scientific Informatics

Longitudinal Research Infrastructure

Research infrastructure designed to support repeated participant data collection, longitudinal study operations, secure data integration, metadata governance, provenance, and reproducible analysis.

Documented contribution: development of a study data management framework and platform design addressing secure intake, metadata governance, provenance, and version controlled analytical workflows.

Study design

Proposed research directions: repeated participant collection and integration of behavioral, environmental, biological, and clinical streams are built into the study design as the foundation for the production platform or participant data collection. No participant information, internal protocols, or security details are included.

Scientific Informatics

Scientific Knowledge Engineering

Methods for systematically discovering, extracting, organizing, connecting, and reviewing scientific information while preserving links between claims, source material, and expert interpretation.

Documented contributions: API research discovery and acquisition, text and data mining, metadata normalization, source extraction, and knowledge organization.

Implemented workflows and evaluated methods

Documented workflows include de-duplication, feature reconciliation, source linked research tracking, recommendation, and structured progress updates. Retrieval augmented generation (RAG) and language model approaches were evaluated for extraction, summarization, and trend identification. Human review is essential; generated text and inferred relationships are not independent scientific evidence. No internal research tracking resources or unpublished conclusions are linked here.

PubChem Chemical Space Explorer

Compare sampled public chemical collections through molecular features, structural similarity, and carefully bounded group-label model evaluation. Source evidence and calculated results remain distinguishable.

Evidence: public API integration, reproducible sampling, RDKit analysis, validation design, and versioned report exports. Status: local Streamlit application; no public runtime or dataset is hosted on this portfolio.
